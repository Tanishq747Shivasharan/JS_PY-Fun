Naruto Shippuden Episode 450 – "Rival" (Detailed Summary)

Note: This episode is part of the "Jiraiya Shinobi Handbook: The Tale of Naruto the Hero" filler arc, which takes place in Tsunade's Infinite Tsukuyomi dream. It is not part of the main canon storyline, although it borrows elements from the manga and reimagines them.

The Final Battle Begins

The episode continues the climactic battle between Naruto Uzumaki and Sasuke Uchiha.

Naruto has one goal: bring Sasuke back to the Hidden Leaf Village. Sasuke, however, believes that the shinobi world is fundamentally broken. He intends to change it by destroying the existing system, even if that means standing against Naruto and everyone else.

The two acknowledge each other as equals but cannot agree on how peace should be achieved.

Naruto's Determination

Naruto repeatedly tells Sasuke that they are friends and that he refuses to give up on him.

He explains that:

They understand each other's loneliness.
They have both suffered tremendous pain.
Breaking their bond would only create more hatred.

Naruto fights not because he wants to defeat Sasuke, but because he wants to save him.

Sasuke's Beliefs

Sasuke reveals why he rejects Naruto's ideals.

He believes:

The current shinobi system creates endless wars.
Villages sacrifice innocent people for political stability.
The only way to achieve lasting peace is through revolution.

Although Sasuke admits Naruto is the closest person to him, he concludes that Naruto is also the greatest obstacle to his plans.

The Battle Escalates

The fight becomes increasingly destructive.

Both use powerful techniques, including:

Shadow Clones
Rasengan
Chidori
Taijutsu
Large-scale chakra attacks

Neither gains a decisive advantage. Their attacks collide repeatedly, creating massive explosions that devastate the battlefield.

Throughout the battle, the episode flashes back to:

Their days at the Ninja Academy.
Missions as members of Team 7.
Their early rivalry.
Moments when they fought side by side.

These memories remind viewers that their conflict is emotional as much as physical.

Naruto Refuses to Give Up

Even after suffering heavy injuries, Naruto continues fighting.

He insists:

Sasuke is still his friend.
He will never abandon him.
He would rather keep reaching out than let hatred decide the future.

Naruto's unwavering determination contrasts sharply with Sasuke's cold resolve.

Sasuke's Decision

Near the end of the battle, Sasuke begins reflecting on everything that has happened:

Itachi's sacrifices.
Naruto's persistence.
The bonds he once shared with Team 7.

Although he remains determined to reshape the world, Naruto's words begin to affect him.

Eventually, Sasuke agrees to return to Konoha—but makes it clear that he is not returning simply to resume life as before. He intends to change the village and the shinobi system according to his own ideals.

Ending

The episode concludes the Jiraiya Ninja Scrolls alternate-story arc. It serves as an emotional resolution to the dream-world version of Naruto and Sasuke's rivalry before the anime returns to the main storyline with the next arc.

Key Themes
Friendship vs. ideology
Breaking the cycle of hatred
Rivalry built on mutual understanding
Different visions of peace
The importance of bonds over revenge
